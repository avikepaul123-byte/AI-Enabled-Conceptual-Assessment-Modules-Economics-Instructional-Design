Fit the Line.

A 25-question challenge on the Classical Linear Regression Model — assumptions, estimators, and the diagnostics that keep OLS honest. Every right answer scores. No penalty for a guess.

How this works

  • 25 questions, one screen at a time — difficulty rises from easy to hard as you go.
  • Each question is multiple-choice with a 30-second timer. No answer when time runs out just scores zero — nothing is deducted.
  • Every correct answer earns 10 points — 250 points on the table, no negative marking anywhere.
  • At the end, your scorecard shows your score and a grade with a comment on your CLRM command.
  • Tap Review Answers afterward to see which questions you got right or wrong, plus the model answer and reasoning for every question.
